# Examples

Finished Motion Studio projects you can watch, open, change and re-render.

## Motion Studio launch film

**[▶ Watch: motion-studio-launch-film.mp4](motion-studio-launch-film.mp4)** · 47 s · 2560×1440

Made entirely by Claude Code inside Motion Studio, from a single script:

- creative direction and a 12-scene storyboard with shots
- every animation cued to the spoken words: kinetic type, a self-building dashboard, a chart of the film's own data, a cursor dragging a cue onto a word, a motion path, a zoom into a screen recording
- frosted-glass panels over drifting light and three painterly backdrops (AI-generated)
- ElevenLabs voice-over and music bed, plus sound effects timed to the words

**Open it yourself:** start the studio (`npm run dev`), click **Import package** on the dashboard and choose
[`motion-studio-launch-film.zip`](motion-studio-launch-film.zip). It becomes a new project in your library:
open any scene to see how it is built, change it, and render your own version.

**Share your own:** Render page → **Project package** → download the `.zip`.
